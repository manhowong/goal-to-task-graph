# Goal Planning Guidance

## Decomposition principles
- Break a large goal into concrete, verifiable actions.
- Prefer short tasks that have a clear completion condition.
- Group tasks by dependencies, urgency, and risk.

## What to consider
- Constraints, regulations, and operational requirements.
- Deadlines, blockers, and resource availability.
- Dependencies between tasks and required sequencing.
- Safety, validation, and rollback considerations.

## Quality checks
- Tasks should be specific enough to execute without extra interpretation.
- Each task should be observable and testable.
- Avoid vague or multi-purpose actions that hide uncertainty.
